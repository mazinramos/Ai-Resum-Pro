Frequently asked questions

Is my resume really ATS-friendly?

Templates follow ATS-readability best practices — clear headings, linear text, no scrambled columns. No tool can promise 100% compatibility with every system, but nothing here works against you.

Do I need a profile photo?

No. It's optional on every template, and left out entirely on ATS-friendly ones. Norms around photos vary by market and role — use your judgment.

What happens to my data?

Your resume is tied to your account and only you can access, edit or delete it. If you're not signed in, it's saved locally in your browser only.

Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes — cancel whenever you like from your account. You keep Premium features until the end of the billing period you already paid for.
